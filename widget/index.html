Frequently
Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from practice directors and clinic managers about onboarding Ellen.

Does Ellen replace my staff? +
No. Ellen is designed to assist your team, not replace them. She handles after-hours inquiries, answers repetitive logistical questions (hours, accepted insurances, practice focus), and gathers prospective client preferences. Your clinical and administrative staff always retains final review over therapist assignment and appointment confirmation.
How long does setup take? +
Most practice builds are ready to preview in 2 to 3 business days. We train Ellen on your practice website, clinician biographies, accepted insurance plans, and published policies so she is grounded in your real information before she ever interacts with a visitor.
Can Ellen be customized to our specific therapists and specialties? +
Yes. Ellen is configured specifically around your roster of clinicians, their individual focus areas (e.g. CBT, trauma, couples, adolescents), and their current caseload availability, rather than following a one-size-fits-all script.
What happens if a visitor mentions a crisis? +
Safety guardrails are hardcoded into Ellen's routing architecture. If crisis, self-harm, or emergency language is detected, Ellen immediately provides established crisis intervention resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, and 911) and stops standard intake dialogue.
Is there a free demo before we commit to anything? +
Yes. We build a functional demonstration of Ellen trained on your practice's public content so you can test her conversations directly before making any commitment.
How is our practice's and clients' data handled? +
We operate under standard administrative HIPAA safeguards and industry-standard encryption. Visitor inquiries and contact information are transmitted securely and delivered directly to your practice without unauthorized sharing.
Does Ellen integrate with our existing scheduling or CRM tools? +
Ellen can log inquiries and intake requests directly into Google Sheets, send instant notifications, or connect with practice management workflows via secure webhooks without disrupting your existing operations.
