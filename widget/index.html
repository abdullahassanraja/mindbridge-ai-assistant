Frequently
Asked Questions

Have more questions? Ellen is online 24/7 to answer anything about services, fees, or therapist match.

How quickly can I be matched with a therapist? +
Most clients are matched and scheduled within 24 to 48 hours. Ellen can check immediate counselor availability and reserve your preferred day and time in real time.
Do you accept health insurance or provide superbills? +
Yes, we work with several major PPO health plans and provide comprehensive itemized superbills for out-of-network reimbursement. Ellen can verify your plan coverage during intake.
Is my conversation with Ellen completely confidential? +
Absolutely. MindBridge operates under strict HIPAA administrative guidelines with 256-bit encryption. Your disclosures are shared solely with the clinician preparing for your session.
What is the difference between Ellen and a human counselor? +
Ellen is our administrative AI assistant. She provides 24/7 intake support, answers logistical questions, and matches you with clinicians. She does not diagnose or provide medical therapy.
Can I choose between in-person and telehealth sessions? +
Yes, we offer both in-person appointments at our tranquil clinic and encrypted, high-definition telehealth sessions across the state.
What if I need immediate crisis support? +
If you or a loved one are experiencing acute distress or suicidal thoughts, please dial or text 988 for the National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or call 911 immediately.
