✦ Questions & Clinical Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral to start therapy? +
No, you can reach out directly — no physician referral is required to begin therapy at MindBridge Wellness.
How do I know which therapist is right for me? +
Our AI assistant can suggest a tailored match based on your goals and preferences, and our clinical intake team confirms the final assignment based on specialty fit and availability. You are also welcome to request a specific clinician by name.
Do you accept insurance and offer sliding scale rates? +
Yes, we accept several major insurance plans. Our administrative team verifies your specific mental health benefits before your first session. For self-pay clients, we offer sliding scale options based on qualifying circumstances.
Is online therapy (telehealth) as effective as in-person? +
Research consistently demonstrates telehealth is just as effective as in-person therapy for many concerns including anxiety, depression, and stress management. Video sessions are conducted through a secure, HIPAA-compliant platform.
What happens during the first session? +
Your therapist will spend the first session understanding your history, current challenges, and personal goals. It is also an open opportunity for you to ask questions and assess clinical fit.
Can this AI assistant diagnose me or provide clinical advice? +
No. Ellen, our AI assistant, is designed strictly to answer practice questions, explain services, and assist with scheduling. Clinical diagnosis and treatment are exclusively provided by our licensed therapists.
Crisis & Emergency Support: MindBridge Wellness is an outpatient practice and does not provide emergency intervention. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) 24/7, or dial 911 immediately.
