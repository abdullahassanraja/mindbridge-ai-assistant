# Policies

## Confidentiality

Everything discussed in therapy is confidential and protected under HIPAA and applicable state law, with limited exceptions required by law (such as risk of harm to self or others, or suspected abuse of a child, elder, or dependent adult). Your therapist will review these limits with you in your first session.

## Cancellation Policy

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed appointments, may be subject to a cancellation fee. Our team can provide the exact fee amount.

## New Client Process

1. Initial inquiry (via phone, website, or this assistant)
2. Brief intake conversation to understand your needs
3. Therapist matching based on specialty and availability
4. Scheduling of first appointment
5. Intake paperwork completed before or at the first session
6. First session focuses on history-gathering and goal-setting

## Insurance & Payment

We accept several major insurance plans. Clients are responsible for verifying their specific mental health benefits, though our administrative team is happy to assist with this. Self-pay clients may ask about our sliding scale options. Payment is due at the time of service unless other arrangements have been made.

## Telehealth Policy

Video sessions are conducted through a secure, HIPAA-compliant platform. Clients must be located in a private space and, for licensing reasons, physically present in a state where their therapist is licensed to practice at the time of the session.

Clients have the right to ask questions about their treatment, seek a second opinion, request a different therapist, and discontinue services at any time. Concerns about care can be directed to our office manager.
