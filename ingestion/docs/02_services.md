# Our Services

## Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions focused on your personal goals, whether that's managing anxiety or depression, working through a difficult life transition, building self-esteem, or developing healthier coping strategies. Sessions are typically 50 minutes, held weekly or biweekly depending on your needs.

**Common reasons clients seek individual therapy:**
- Anxiety and excessive worry
- Depression and low mood
- Stress management
- Grief and loss
- Life transitions (career change, relocation, divorce)
- Self-esteem and identity
- Trauma processing
- Burnout

## Couples Therapy

Support for partners looking to improve communication, resolve recurring conflict, rebuild trust, or navigate a major relationship transition. Sessions are typically 60–75 minutes.

**Common reasons couples seek therapy:**
- Communication breakdowns
- Trust and infidelity recovery
- Pre-marital counseling
- Navigating major life changes together
- Recurring conflict patterns
- Intimacy concerns

## Family Therapy

Sessions designed to help family members understand each other better, resolve conflict, and strengthen relationships. Useful for families navigating a child's behavioral concerns, blended family adjustment, or communication difficulties.

## Teen & Young Adult Counseling

Specialized support for adolescents (13+) and young adults (18–25) navigating identity development, academic or social stress, family conflict, or the transition to independence.

## Group Therapy

Periodically offered themed groups (e.g., anxiety management, grief support). Availability varies by season — ask our team for current group openings.

## What We Do Not Provide

- Psychiatric medication management (we can refer you to a psychiatrist if needed)
- Emergency or crisis intervention services
- Court-ordered evaluations or custody assessments
- In-patient or residential treatment

## Session Fees & Insurance

MindBridge Wellness accepts several major insurance plans and also offers self-pay rates with a sliding scale for qualifying clients. Our team will verify your specific insurance benefits before your first session. Contact us directly for current rates, as these are confirmed by our administrative team rather than this assistant.
