# Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a referral to start therapy?**
No, you can reach out directly — no physician referral is required.

**How do I know which therapist is right for me?**
Our assistant can suggest a good starting match based on what you share about what you're looking for, and our clinical team makes the final assignment based on availability and fit. You're also welcome to request a specific therapist by name.

**What's the difference between individual and couples therapy for relationship issues?**
Individual therapy focuses on your own patterns and experience of the relationship; couples therapy involves both partners together, focusing on shared communication and dynamics. Some clients start with individual sessions before transitioning to couples work, or do both concurrently with different therapists.

**Do you accept insurance?**
We accept several major insurance plans. Because coverage varies by plan, our administrative team verifies your specific benefits before your first session.

**What if I don't have insurance?**
We offer self-pay rates and a sliding scale for qualifying clients. Reach out to our team for current rates.

**How soon can I get an appointment?**
Availability varies by therapist. Our team will share current openings once we understand which type of support you're looking for.

**Can I switch therapists if it's not a good fit?**
Yes. Fit matters, and you're welcome to request a different therapist at any time — just let our team know.

**Do you see clients outside of your state?**
Therapists can only see clients who are physically located in a state where the therapist is licensed. Let us know your location and we'll confirm availability.

**Is online therapy as effective as in-person?**
Research generally supports telehealth as an effective format for many types of therapy. Some clients prefer in-person, especially for couples or family sessions — this is a personal preference we're happy to discuss.

**What happens in a first session?**
Your therapist will spend most of the first session learning about your history, current concerns, and goals for therapy. It's also a chance for you to ask questions and see if the therapist feels like a good fit.

**Can this AI assistant diagnose me or tell me what's wrong?**
No. This assistant cannot provide a diagnosis, clinical assessment, or treatment advice. It can help you understand our services and connect with the right therapist — but assessment and diagnosis only happen with a licensed therapist.

**Is this assistant a real person?**
No — this is an AI assistant designed to answer general questions and help route your inquiry. You can request to speak with a member of our human team at any time.
