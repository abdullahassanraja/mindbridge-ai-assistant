# Our Therapists

## Dr. Elena Marsh, PsyD — Licensed Clinical Psychologist

**Specialties:** Anxiety disorders, depression, trauma (EMDR-trained), life transitions

**Approach:** Dr. Marsh uses a trauma-informed, client-centered approach blending CBT and EMDR. She works primarily with adults navigating anxiety, past trauma, and major life changes.

**Best fit for clients who:** Want a structured, goal-oriented approach; are processing past trauma; are dealing with persistent anxiety or panic.

**Session formats:** In-person and video

---

## Marcus Reyes, LMFT — Licensed Marriage & Family Therapist

**Specialties:** Couples therapy, family therapy, communication issues, blended family dynamics

**Approach:** Marcus uses Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) to help couples and families understand the emotional patterns driving conflict, and rebuild secure connection.

**Best fit for clients who:** Are seeking couples or family counseling; want to improve communication; are navigating a relationship transition (engagement, blended family, separation).

**Session formats:** In-person and video

---

## Priya Nair, LCSW — Licensed Clinical Social Worker

**Specialties:** Depression, grief and loss, stress management, young adult/college-age concerns

**Approach:** Priya integrates ACT (Acceptance and Commitment Therapy) with mindfulness-based techniques, especially for clients navigating grief, burnout, or identity questions in early adulthood.

**Best fit for clients who:** Are grieving a loss; are experiencing burnout or chronic stress; are young adults adjusting to new life stages; prefer a warm, mindfulness-oriented style.

**Session formats:** In-person, video, and phone (existing clients)

---

## Jordan Whitfield, LPC — Licensed Professional Counselor

**Specialties:** Teen and adolescent counseling, identity development, academic/social stress, family conflict involving teens

**Approach:** Jordan works collaboratively with teens and their families, using a strengths-based approach that respects the teen's autonomy while involving family as appropriate.

**Best fit for clients who:** Are teenagers (13+) or parents seeking support for a teen; are navigating identity or social stress; want a therapist experienced in adolescent development.

**Session formats:** In-person and video

## A Note on Matching

Our AI assistant can help suggest which therapist's specialties may align with what you're looking for, based on the information you share. However, **final therapist assignment is always made by our clinical team**, taking into account availability, clinical fit, and your preferences. The assistant's suggestion is a starting point for that conversation, not a final decision.
