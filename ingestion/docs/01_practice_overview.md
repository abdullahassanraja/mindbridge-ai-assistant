# About MindBridge Wellness

MindBridge Wellness is a counseling and mental wellness practice dedicated to helping individuals, couples, and families navigate life's challenges with compassionate, evidence-based care. We believe that seeking support is a sign of strength, and we're committed to making that first step as easy and welcoming as possible.

## Our Mission

To provide accessible, personalized mental health support in a safe, non-judgmental space — helping clients build resilience, self-understanding, and healthier relationships.

## Our Approach

Our therapists draw from a range of evidence-based modalities, including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), and trauma-informed care. We tailor our approach to each client's unique needs rather than applying a one-size-fits-all method.

## Who We Serve

- Adults navigating anxiety, depression, stress, or life transitions
- Couples working through communication challenges or relationship conflict
- Families seeking to improve dynamics and communication
- Individuals processing grief, trauma, or major life changes
- Young adults and college students adjusting to new responsibilities

## Session Formats

- In-person sessions at our office
- Secure video (telehealth) sessions
- Phone sessions (available for existing clients)

## Location & Hours
